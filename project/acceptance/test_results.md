## What is not covered

Stated rather than left to be discovered:

- **Screenshot-diff specs** were trimmed under the pre-agreed scope-trim order. Visual
  parity against the evaluated build is evidenced by side-by-side captures in
  `project/parity/` instead of by assertion.
- **No test runs against the target environment.** Every result here is from a local Kubernetes
  cluster in CI, which is the fallback the TDD states for pending sandbox access.
- **No test exercises the live model gateway.** `MODEL_ENDPOINT`, `MODEL_NAME` and
  `MODEL_API_KEY` are environment-specific and absent from the repository; the services
  answer from fixtures without them and log which mode they are in at startup.
- **The 200 ms interaction budget is measured on a synthetic 2,000-message thread**,
  because the delivered corpus’s largest real thread is 18 messages. The budget itself
  is the TDD’s (Task 5); the synthetic thread is how it is made testable.
